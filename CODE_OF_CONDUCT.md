# Code of Conduct

Juxt-lang follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/juxt-lang/juxt/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of Conduct", or by messaging a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove content, and warn, suspend or ban participants, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
